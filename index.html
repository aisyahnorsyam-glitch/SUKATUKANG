Meet Sukatukang

Every business has a story.
We help bring it to life.

A cart can be the start of something bigger. At Sukatukang, we bring woodcraft, thoughtful design and everyday function together to create a business space that feels like you.

Based in Batu Pahat, Johor, Sukatukang works with clients in Malaysia, Singapore and Brunei. From your first cart to an event counter, every commission is shaped around your needs.
